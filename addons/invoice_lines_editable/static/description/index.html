Invoice Lines Editable

Manual Journal Items for Invoices (Odoo 18)

Enable full control over journal items on invoices. Tick Manual Journal Items, edit any debit/credit, click Balance with Receivable / Payable, then Save & Confirm.

  • Stops auto-recompute of receivable / tax lines
  • One-click exact balance
  • Clear error showing the exact difference
  • Compatible with other custom accounting modules
